What it does

Six modules. One architecture — whether it's a plant or a laptop.

01

Universal Tagging

Every asset — fixed or mobile — gets a QR or NFC tag the moment it's registered. No asset type needs custom setup.

02

Access Control

Only the assigned person can log an action on an asset. Every other attempt is recorded, not silently blocked.

03

Activity Timeline

Every scan builds a full history — who touched what, when, and what they did to it.

04

Maintenance Scheduling

Time-based or usage-based rules trigger reminders automatically — water a plant, service a machine, sharpen a tool.

05

Movement & Theft Alerts

Assets that leave their expected zone without an authorized log trigger a real-time alert.

06

Reports & Dashboard

A live view of every asset's status, location, and history — filterable by zone, employee, or asset type.
